🏍 Traffic Rider: Bike Delivery

A city-based bike delivery simulation focused on mission flow and traffic interaction without narrative systems. Built as a streamlined version of an open-world delivery architecture with emphasis on performance, scalability, and continuous gameplay.

What I Built

  • Custom bike controller tuned for responsive acceleration, braking, and lane-level precision
  • Traffic-integrated delivery mission system with dynamic pickup and drop-off sequencing
  • Vehicle interaction layer aligned with city traffic rules and route-based objective tracking
  • Reusable mission definitions enabling rapid level and objective iteration
  • Centralized gameplay flow manager handling retries, rewards, and mission transitions

Core Systems

  • Continuous delivery loop: assign mission → navigate traffic → complete handoff → chain next task
  • Route and checkpoint validation pipeline for reliable mission completion events
  • Difficulty scaling through delivery distance, timing windows, and traffic density
  • Session progression model designed for short, repeatable high-engagement gameplay cycles

AI & NPC Architecture

  • Manager-driven NPC orchestration for predictable updates across dense city regions
  • Traffic agent behavior states for cruise, stop, yield, and obstruction reactions
  • Spawn balancing based on player zone and speed to maintain consistent world activity
  • Lightweight AI profiles for stable simulation under mission-heavy runtime conditions

Performance & Optimization

  • Object pooling for NPCs, traffic vehicles, mission props, and effects to minimize allocations
  • Distance-based activation and recycling to control CPU load in open city traversal
  • Centralized manager logic replacing per-object heavy update patterns
  • Data-driven mission and traffic tuning for scalable content without scene duplication

Tools & Technologies

  • Unity (C#)
  • Traffic simulation system
  • Custom bike controller framework
  • Object pooling architecture
  • ScriptableObject-driven gameplay configuration

My Role

  • Sole developer responsible for architecture, implementation, and gameplay balancing
  • Designed mission flow, traffic interaction systems, and controller behavior
  • Built manager-driven AI and pooling systems for stable mobile performance
  • Executed profiling and optimization passes to maintain continuous gameplay responsiveness
