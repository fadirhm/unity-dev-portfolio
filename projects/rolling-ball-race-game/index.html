🎱 Rolling Ball Race Game

Arcade Puzzle Racing

Level-based 3D ball-runner: swipe to steer along narrow sky paths, dodge hazards, collect coins, and climb a leaderboard—blending balance-ball action with light slide-puzzle beats across themed worlds.

Gameplay

Rollance-style going-balls flow: steer the ball left and right through city, temple, and sky-high tracks; difficulty ramps with tighter turns and stunt sections; coins unlock ball looks and boosts; some levels fold in block-style puzzle gates before you race on.

What I owned

  • Full gameplay mechanics design for a level-based structure (~30–40 levels).
  • Three modes: Story, Jungle, and multiplayer-style racing vs NPCs.
  • Complete level design pass, plus player and environment selection flows.

My role

  • Solo ownership of core mechanic design, modes, and level authoring for this title at GlixenStudios.
  • Shipped to Google Play as part of the studio’s Android slate.
