🌆 Gangster Mafia War: Open World

Live open-world action title where I owned stability and ANR reduction: profiling with Firebase Crashlytics and Logcat, fixing startup and gameplay spikes, and tightening main-thread work so the game stayed within store thresholds.

Contribution

  • Brought ANRs back under the Play Console threshold by eliminating freezes and long stalls on the main thread
  • Resolved crash-on-open and intermittent gameplay crashes traced through stack traces and device logs
  • Staged initialization of existing systems so heavy work did not hit the main thread all at once on launch
  • Removed redundant per-frame and startup work that caused hitches during play
  • Simplified hot paths: cut unnecessary loops and iterations, kept scripts efficient and easier to maintain

How I approached it

  • Used Firebase Crashlytics and Logcat to group issues by frequency and severity before changing code
  • Profiled frame spikes and loading phases to find work that belonged off the main thread or in smaller steps
  • Validated fixes against the same profiling signals to confirm ANRs and jank dropped measurably

Tools & technologies

  • Unity (C#)
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Android Logcat & Play Console ANR reports

My role

  • Major contribution: performance and stability on a live codebase — not solo authorship of the original title
  • Focused on ANR prevention, crash fixes, and sustainable initialization patterns
