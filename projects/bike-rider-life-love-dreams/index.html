🎮 Bike Rider Life: Love & Dreams

A city-based open-world experience where players complete delivery missions while progressing through a relationship-driven system. The gameplay blends exploration, mission flow, and interactive decision-making.

Core Gameplay

  • Delivery missions across an open-world city
  • Exploration using multiple vehicle types
  • Relationship system with choices and interactions
  • Activities such as gifting and shared rides

Delivery System

  • Orders managed through in-game mobile phone
  • Accept, pick up, and deliver missions
  • Continuous order updates to maintain gameplay loop

AI & NPC System

  • Manager-driven pedestrian system controlling all NPCs
  • Spline-based pathing for optimized movement
  • State machine-driven behavior transitions
  • Object pooling for efficient NPC reuse
  • Distance-based recycling of far NPCs near player
  • NPC detaches and follows player during interaction
  • Re-subscribes back into system after interaction
  • NavMesh used only as fallback when NPC gets stuck

Tools & Systems

  • Mobile Traffic System
  • Easy Minimap System
  • Dialogue System

  • Implemented delivery and interaction systems
  • Optimized performance for large open-world environment
