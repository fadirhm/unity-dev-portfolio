🛒 Supermart Store Simulator Game

Port and rescue of a PC-oriented supermarket simulator into a shippable Android (and later iOS) product: repairing a broken project, replacing unsuitable plugins, rebuilding content for mobile budgets, and delivering touch controls, tutorials, and performance work for a broad API 24+ device target.

Context

  • Received a damaged Unity project originally aimed at PC / Steam: thousands of errors, missing libraries, plugins, and assets — not playable in the editor
  • Goal: make it run, then optimize and ship on mobile within tight hardware limits

Recovery & porting

  • Restored missing plugins and swapped or removed ones that were not viable on Unity + Android
  • Iterated until the project loaded and played; then addressed PC-centric assumptions scene by scene
  • First gameplay scene initially took ~10–15 minutes to load in-editor due to mesh, shader, and script issues plus asset bloat
  • Replaced heavy models, removed unnecessary shaders, and converted inappropriate mesh colliders to simpler colliders
  • Sequenced scene initialization to spread cost instead of one massive blocking load

Optimization & gameplay scope

  • Replaced per-frame Update misuse with lighter, custom update groups where appropriate
  • Full reskin and asset compression; tuned batches and rendering with baking and culling where it helped
  • Pooling for NPCs and products to cut allocation spikes and keep framerate stable
  • After profiling, removed the paint system (too many textures) and trimmed furniture and product variety to what gameplay required for low-end targets
  • Tuned for API 24+ across a wide range of devices with conservative quality settings

Controls, onboarding & launch

  • Rebuilt PC interactions for touch: mapped everything through the new Input System for consistent mobile controls
  • Authored a full in-game day of tutorial so players could learn the loop on phone
  • Roughly one to one and a half months to Play Store launch, then App Store for iOS

My role

  • Major contribution: end-to-end technical ownership of port, optimization, UX for mobile, and shipping — on an existing design, not a greenfield solo title

Tools & technologies

  • Unity (C#)
  • Unity Input System
  • Android & iOS build pipelines
