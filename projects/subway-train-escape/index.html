🚇 Subway Train Escape!

Puzzle Strategy Casual

Optimization and ANR work on a level-based puzzle title: sequential initialization, lighter update loops, and moving level content off a single massive load so the main thread stayed responsive across dozens of levels.

Gameplay

Offline 3D track puzzles: tap trains to clear traffic, dodge collisions and hazards, and survive escalating “boss” puzzle beats. Collect map pieces over time to unlock a separate scavenger-hunt mode where you hunt hidden objects.

Contribution

  • Resolved ANRs tied to startup and early-game stalls, including main-thread contention during the first moments of play
  • Replaced unnecessary per-frame work with custom update groups where the design did not need full Update every frame
  • Identified that preloading ~50–80 levels at once was overloading initialization; shifted to per-level data and runtime spawning
  • Stored level definitions in ScriptableObjects and spawned the active level into the gameplay scene at load time — keeping memory and init cost bounded
  • Noted that Addressables or additive loading would be a strong next step; the ScriptableObject + spawn approach shipped first and stabilized the build

My role

  • Major contribution: performance and stability on an existing codebase — not the original solo author of the title

Tools & technologies

  • Unity (C#)
  • ScriptableObjects for level data
  • Android profiling & ANR reports
